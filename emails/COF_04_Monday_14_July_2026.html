Alpha GPC and acetylcholine

Alpha GPC (alpha-glycerylphosphorylcholine) is one of the most bioavailable forms of choline available. Choline is the precursor to acetylcholine - the neurotransmitter most directly associated with learning, memory formation, and focused attention.

Most people are mildly choline deficient. The symptoms are subtle: slight cognitive fog, difficulty sustaining focus, tasks taking longer than they should. Alpha GPC at 300mg - the dose in Keto Brainz - has been shown in clinical studies to significantly raise brain acetylcholine levels within hours of ingestion.
