Sunday evening. The week behind you, the week ahead not yet started. This is one of the most powerful windows of the week for your hormonal health - if you use it intentionally.

The Sunday reset protocol

Cortisol follows a weekly rhythm as much as a daily one. Monday morning cortisol spikes are driven partly by the anticipatory stress of the week ahead that begins forming in your nervous system Sunday afternoon.

A simple Sunday evening ritual can meaningfully buffer this. Not by eliminating the stress response but by giving your body the nutritional and physiological resources to handle it.

Dr Anna recommends: a warm bath with magnesium flakes to support transdermal absorption of this critical mineral. A scoop of Mighty Maca in warm water or herbal tea. Ten minutes without a screen before bed. Nothing complicated. Everything evidence-based.

The weeks that go well usually start the Sunday evening before them.

Your body does its most important hormonal work between 10pm and 2am. Set it up to succeed tonight.
