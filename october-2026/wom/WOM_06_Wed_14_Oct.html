How light governs energy and motivation in women

Serotonin synthesis in the brain is directly proportional to light exposure. As October reduces daily light hours, serotonin production falls. Dopamine, the neurotransmitter of motivation, drive and reward-seeking, is linked to serotonin through shared precursor pathways. When serotonin falls, dopamine signalling typically follows. For perimenopausal women, who already have reduced oestrogen-mediated serotonin support, the autumn light reduction hits a system that is already operating below its younger capacity. The result is a motivational and energy deficit that is neurochemical in origin and requires neurochemical solutions, not motivational ones.
