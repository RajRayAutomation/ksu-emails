The science

Why October is the critical bone density intervention window

Vitamin D, which is essential for calcium absorption into bone, is produced by skin exposure to UV-B light. In the UK and northern latitudes, UV-B levels fall below the threshold for vitamin D synthesis from October through to March. This means that vitamin D stores built during summer begin depleting in October. Women who enter October with adequate vitamin D status have a buffer. Women who enter October already borderline, which is the majority of perimenopausal women in the UK, begin the winter with insufficient calcium absorption capacity from the first week. Starting vitamin D supplementation now preserves the absorption mechanism through the winter that poses the highest bone loss risk.
