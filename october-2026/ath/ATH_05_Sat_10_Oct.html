The science

Concurrent training and the autumn strength window

Research on concurrent training, combining endurance and strength work in the same training block, consistently shows that the interference effect between the two is minimised when strength work is prioritised in the early base phase. Testosterone is naturally elevated in autumn in response to falling daylight. Growth hormone production is higher. The hormonal environment in October is measurably more anabolic than in summer heat stress. Athletes who add two strength sessions per week in October convert that hormonal environment into muscle fibre recruitment improvements that directly benefit their endurance economy by spring.
