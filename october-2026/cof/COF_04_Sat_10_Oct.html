BDNF, seasonal neuroplasticity and the October window

BDNF, Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor, is the primary regulator of neuroplasticity in the adult brain. Its production is stimulated by exercise, cold exposure, caloric restriction and novel environmental demands. All four of these are elevated in the early weeks of October relative to stable summer conditions. Lion's Mane contains hericenones and erinacines that directly stimulate NGF and BDNF production through pathways that are additive to these environmental stimuli.
