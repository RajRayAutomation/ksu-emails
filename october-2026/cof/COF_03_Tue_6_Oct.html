Chlorogenic acids and thermogenic fat metabolism

Chlorogenic acids, present in significantly higher concentrations in high-altitude coffee than in commodity coffee, have been studied for their effect on fat metabolism in multiple clinical trials. The mechanism involves inhibition of glucose-6-phosphatase, which reduces hepatic glucose release and shifts the body toward fat oxidation as a primary fuel. In cold conditions, where the body is already mobilising fat for thermogenesis, this effect is amplified. The result is that high-altitude coffee consumed in cold morning conditions produces a more pronounced metabolic benefit than the same coffee consumed at room temperature in July.

Fun fact: High-altitude coffee contains 25 to 40% more chlorogenic acid than lowland-grown varieties, due to the slower ripening process and higher UV radiation at altitude. Roast level also matters.
