Why clean coffee matters and where the movement is going

The specialty coffee movement has been transforming the industry for twenty years — from commodity to origin to practice. The clean coffee movement, which focuses on what is not in the coffee as much as what is, is the next evolution. Mycotoxin testing, pesticide-free cultivation, heavy metal monitoring, traceability to the specific farm and lot. These are becoming the standard for the premium market, driven by consumers who understand that clean coffee is not just about flavour. It is about what chronic low-level exposure to contamination does to inflammation, gut health and cognitive function over years and decades of daily consumption.

Therme Bucharest came to KSU this summer because they understood this. One of the world's leading health and wellbeing organisations wanted the coffee on their counters to reflect the same standard as everything else in their environment. Purity Coffee was the answer. KSU was the partner. That conversation is not the end of the Therme story. Watch for what comes next.

Fun fact: The specialty coffee market was valued at approximately $85 billion globally in 2023. The clean coffee subcategory — defined by third-party testing and transparent sourcing — is growing at approximately three times the rate of the broader specialty market as consumer awareness of mycotoxin contamination increases.

Nearly a decade. Something significant coming in February. You will hear it here first.
