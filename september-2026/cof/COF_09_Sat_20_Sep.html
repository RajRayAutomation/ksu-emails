Most decaf coffee is a compromise. The decaffeination process — typically using chemical solvents — strips not just the caffeine but a significant proportion of the chlorogenic acids that make coffee worth drinking from a health perspective.

Purity Coffee Decaf is different in a specific and verifiable way. It uses the Swiss Water Process — a purely physical, water-based decaffeination method that removes 99.9% of caffeine without solvents, while preserving the chlorogenic acid content and flavour profile almost intact.

The science

Why caffeine timing matters and what the Swiss Water Process actually does

Caffeine has a half-life of approximately five to seven hours in the average adult — meaning a coffee consumed at 2pm still has half its caffeine present at 7 to 9pm. This residual caffeine suppresses adenosine — the sleep-pressure molecule — and reduces slow-wave sleep duration even when sleep onset is not affected. The result is sleep that feels adequate but is less restorative. Purity Coffee Decaf allows the evening ritual — the warmth, the antioxidants, the contemplative pause — without the adenosine suppression that undermines sleep quality.

The evening cup has its own ritual value distinct from the morning one. The morning coffee primes for action. The evening coffee is the deliberate downshift — the signal to the nervous system that the cognitive demands of the day are releasing. Keeping it in place, cleanly and without caffeine, preserves the ritual without the physiological cost.

Fun fact: The Swiss Water Process was developed in Switzerland in the 1930s. It uses green coffee extract — water saturated with coffee soluble compounds except caffeine — to draw caffeine out of the beans through osmosis while preserving other soluble compounds including chlorogenic acids and aromatic precursors.
