The science

L-Theanine and the caffeine-theanine synergy

L-Theanine is an amino acid found almost exclusively in tea leaves. It crosses the blood-brain barrier and increases alpha wave activity — the brainwave pattern associated with relaxed alertness, the same state experienced during meditation. It moderates the excitatory effects of caffeine by antagonising glutamate receptors and promoting GABA activity, reducing the anxiety and jitteriness that caffeine can produce without reducing its alertness-promoting effects. The combination of caffeine and L-Theanine produces greater improvements in sustained attention and accuracy than caffeine alone, with significantly reduced anxiety and a smoother onset and offset.

At 250mg per serving in Keto Brainz, L-Theanine is present at approximately double the dose used in most research studies. Combined with the caffeine from your morning coffee, it produces the focused calm that distinguishes clean cognitive performance from stimulant-driven anxiety.

Fun fact: Green tea — the original source of L-Theanine — has been consumed in Japan for centuries. Japanese researchers first identified L-Theanine's relaxation-promoting effects in 1949. The traditional tea ceremony, with its emphasis on calm alertness, was probably selecting for the L-Theanine effect long before the compound was isolated.
