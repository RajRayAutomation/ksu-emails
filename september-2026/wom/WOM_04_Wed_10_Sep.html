The science

The thyroid-hormone connection in perimenopause

Oestrogen influences thyroid binding globulin production — the protein that carries thyroid hormone in the blood. As oestrogen fluctuates in perimenopause, thyroid binding globulin levels change, affecting how much free thyroid hormone is available to cells even when total levels appear normal. Additionally, progesterone directly supports thyroid function and conversion of T4 to the active T3 form. As progesterone declines in perimenopause, thyroid conversion efficiency typically declines with it. The result is a woman with technically normal thyroid tests who is functionally hypothyroid.
