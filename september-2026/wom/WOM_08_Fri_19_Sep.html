The science

Oestrogen and the brain — what the research shows

Oestrogen has neuroprotective effects that extend far beyond reproduction. It supports the growth and maintenance of synaptic connections, promotes the production of neurotrophic factors including BDNF, modulates neurotransmitter systems including serotonin, dopamine and acetylcholine, and maintains cerebral blood flow. Its decline in perimenopause can produce measurable changes in working memory, verbal recall and processing speed. These changes are typically reversible with hormonal support — which is why the timing of initiation matters enormously.
