Genitourinary syndrome of menopause — what it is and what the options are

Genitourinary syndrome of menopause encompasses vaginal dryness, atrophy, irritation and urinary symptoms affecting over 50% of postmenopausal women. Unlike hot flushes, which typically resolve over time, genitourinary symptoms are progressive without treatment. Oestrogen maintains the thickness, elasticity and lubrication of vaginal tissue. Without it, the tissue becomes thinner and more fragile. DHEA applied directly to vaginal tissue converts locally to oestrogen and testosterone, restoring tissue health without significant systemic absorption.
