The science

The four-mineral electrolyte matrix your muscles actually depend on

Electrolytes carry an electrical charge in solution. Your muscles use this electrical signalling to contract and relax. Four minerals govern this process: sodium regulates fluid balance and nerve signals. Potassium works with sodium to maintain the electrical gradient across muscle cell membranes. Magnesium governs over 300 enzymatic reactions and is directly involved in ATP production. Calcium triggers the actual contraction of muscle fibres. Most commercial sports drinks provide sodium and sometimes potassium. Magnesium and calcium are either absent or present in amounts too small to matter. The result is partial electrolyte replacement that leaves athletes vulnerable exactly when they are most depleted.
