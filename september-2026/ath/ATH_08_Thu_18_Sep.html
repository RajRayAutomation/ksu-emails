The gut-performance connection the research has been building for a decade

The gut microbiome influences athletic performance through multiple pathways. Short-chain fatty acids produced by gut bacteria directly fuel intestinal cells and modulate systemic inflammation. Gut bacteria regulate iron absorption, directly affecting oxygen-carrying capacity. Emerging research shows that specific bacterial strains convert lactate to propionate, turning a metabolic waste product into additional fuel.
