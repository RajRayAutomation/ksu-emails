The science

What ATP actually is and why ketones change the equation

ATP is adenosine triphosphate. The energy currency of every cell in your body. Every muscle contraction, every neural signal runs on it. Your body produces ATP through two pathways: glycolysis from glucose, and oxidative phosphorylation from fats and ketones. The difference is not just efficiency. It is the cost paid in oxygen. Glucose is fast but expensive. Ketones are cleaner, producing 28% more ATP per unit of oxygen consumed. In summer heat, where your body already spends oxygen managing core temperature, that efficiency gap becomes the difference between performing and surviving the session.
