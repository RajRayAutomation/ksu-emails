The science

Inflammation, cytokines and the recovery deficit

Exercise induces the release of pro-inflammatory cytokines that initiate tissue repair. In healthy recovery, anti-inflammatory cytokines follow and inflammation resolves within 24 to 72 hours. When training volume exceeds recovery capacity, pro-inflammatory cytokines accumulate without adequate anti-inflammatory resolution. The result is chronic systemic inflammation that suppresses performance, disrupts sleep, increases injury risk and impairs cognitive function.

Ketones have a direct anti-inflammatory effect independent of their role as fuel. Beta-hydroxybutyrate — the primary ketone body in Real Ketones — inhibits the NLRP3 inflammasome, one of the key drivers of systemic inflammation. This is not a secondary benefit. It is a primary mechanism.

Fun fact: Beta-hydroxybutyrate was identified as an endogenous inhibitor of the NLRP3 inflammasome in 2015 research published in Nature Medicine. The implications for recovery and inflammation management are still being fully understood.
