Tips

How to make a video you will be proud of

1. Face a window. Natural daylight is kinder than any lamp.

2. Prop your phone against a mug or a pile of books and film it upright. No tripod needed.

3. Choose a quiet room and switch off any music or the television.

4. Aim for 30 to 60 seconds. Talk to us the way you would talk to a friend over a cup of tea.

5. Do not write a script. Jot down four prompts instead: your first name, what made you try Julva, how long you have used it and what you have noticed.

6. Share your own experience. The words "for me" are the most powerful ones you can use.

7. Do not aim for perfect. One take is fine. A little laugh or a pause makes it more believable, not less.

Not keen on showing your face? Voice only is perfect. Record yourself speaking while you film your hands, a cup of tea or the product on a shelf. We are just as grateful.
