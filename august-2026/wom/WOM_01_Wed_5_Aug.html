I have been to 51 countries. I have moved fast through most of them. This summer, for the first time in a long time, I moved slowly.

What the river teaches

A river cruise is not a holiday in the ordinary sense. The scenery changes, country by country, but the rhythm stays the same. Slow. Present. Unhurried.

And something happened in my body within the first three days. The cortisol dropped. The sleep deepened. The noise of running a business from a boat quietened into something manageable.

Stillness is not inactivity. It is one of the most powerful hormonal interventions available to you.

Dr Anna has said this for decades. Rest is a biological necessity for hormonal health. The research on cortisol, progesterone and the nervous system is unambiguous on this point.

This August - wherever you are - find one moment of slow. Your hormones will notice.
