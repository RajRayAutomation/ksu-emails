The science

Why easy volume works

Low intensity work builds the aerobic foundation: more capillaries around working muscle, more mitochondria, better use of fat as fuel. These adaptations build over weeks, which is why a patient November pays off in March. The common mistake is making every session medium hard, which is too hard to recover from and too easy to drive big change.

Here is how a simple base week might look. Three or four easy sessions of thirty to sixty minutes, one longer easy session at the weekend and one short strength session. No heroics. The point is to accumulate quality time at an effort that leaves you feeling better at the end than at the start.

The hardest part is ego. Easy pace can feel embarrassingly slow, especially when friends are posting fast sessions. The people who are fastest in spring are usually the ones who were patient in winter. Note how you feel at the end of each easy session. If you finish fresh, you are doing it right.
