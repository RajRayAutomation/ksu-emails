How to shop the sales sensibly

Sales work by creating urgency. Before you buy, ask three questions. Will I use this weekly? Do I know it works for me? Would I buy it at the normal price? If the answer to all three is yes, go ahead. If not, walk away.

Our rule of thumb is simple. Buy what you will use in the next two months, not what you might use someday. Check the delivery date, because a deal that arrives after Christmas is not a deal. And ignore any timer that restarts when you refresh the page.

If you are stocking up, do it once, calmly, and then forget about it. The best sale is the one you do not have to think about again. Spend the energy you save on getting outside for a session.
