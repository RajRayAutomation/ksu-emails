The science

What recovery needs

After hard training your body needs fluid, electrolytes, protein and carbohydrate, and then sleep. Sweat takes sodium, potassium and magnesium with it, and plain water does not put them back. Eating and drinking within the first hour makes it easier to rebuild. None of this is exotic. It is simply easy to skip when life is busy.

Think of recovery as three buckets: refuel, rehydrate and rest. Refuel means a proper meal with protein and carbohydrate within a couple of hours, sooner if you are training again that day. Rehydrate means fluid plus electrolytes, not just water. Rest means sleep, and ideally a short walk or gentle stretch rather than collapsing straight onto the sofa.

It sounds basic because it is. The athletes who recover best are rarely the ones with the fanciest gadgets. They are the ones who do the basics every time. If you only fix one bucket this week, make it the second, because hydration is the one most people forget in winter.
