Holding fitness through the holidays

Short, regular sessions hold aerobic fitness remarkably well. Twenty to thirty minutes, three or four times a week, with one slightly harder effort, is enough to avoid a January reset. The goal for December is to arrive in January feeling like you never stopped.

A realistic December plan has three parts. First, protect your anchor sessions: the two or three you will do whatever happens. Second, keep one slightly harder effort a week so your engine stays awake. Third, plan your recovery as carefully as your training, because holiday meals and late nights are also a load.

If you miss a week, do not try to make it up. Just carry on. Fitness is built over months, not days, and a good November means you have more in the bank than you think.
