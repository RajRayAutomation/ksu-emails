The science

What the estrobolome is

The estrobolome is the collection of gut bacteria that help metabolise oestrogen. When your gut is in good shape, oestrogen is processed and cleared in a balanced way. When it is out of balance, the picture can be harder to predict. Research here is still developing, but the practical advice is encouraging and low risk: more fibre, more variety in plants, fermented foods and less ultra processed food.

Most of us eat the same fifteen or twenty foods on repeat. Adding variety does not need to be complicated. Swap your usual lunch vegetable for a different one. Add a spoonful of seeds to breakfast. Throw a handful of herbs into dinner. Try a new bean or lentil each week. Small, cheap and effective.

Fermented foods such as live yoghurt, kefir, sauerkraut and kimchi add another layer. Introduce them slowly if your gut is sensitive and notice how you feel over a few weeks. The gut changes gradually, so patience matters more than perfection.
