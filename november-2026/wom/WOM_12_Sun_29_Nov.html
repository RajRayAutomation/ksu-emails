A simple December plan

Choose one habit for sleep, one for movement and one for food. A set bedtime, a daily walk and protein at breakfast, for example. Three habits are easier to keep than ten, and each one supports the others. Review at the end of the month and keep what worked.

Habits stick when they are small and attached to something that already happens. Walk after dinner. Protein at breakfast. Lights out at the same time. If you miss a day, restart the next. The goal is not perfection, it is a rhythm you can live with.

December tends to disrupt routines, so lower the bar. Ten minutes of movement counts. One good meal counts. An early night counts. Keep the chain going gently and you will arrive in January ahead of where you would have been.
