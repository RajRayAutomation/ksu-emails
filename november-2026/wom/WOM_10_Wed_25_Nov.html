Why self care sticks when it is simple

The routines that last are the ones that are simple, regular and attached to something you already do. A daily scoop in a morning drink, a ten minute walk after lunch, a consistent bedtime. Buying for yourself is a decision to invest in those routines, not an indulgence.

Think about what really helps you. Perhaps it is a daily supplement that supports your routine, a good pair of walking boots, a book you have been meaning to read or a day off. Put it on the list. You would not hesitate to buy it for a friend.

And if you decide to buy, buy once and well. Choose things you will use, from brands you trust, and leave the rest. That is a kind of self care too.
