The science

How stress and hormones interact

Your body makes cortisol and sex hormones from related building blocks, and your stress response is closely connected to how you feel through the menopause transition. Chronic stress, poor sleep and low blood sugar all make symptoms feel louder. Calming the nervous system through breathing, movement, daylight and good food gives your hormones a steadier backdrop.

A simple December strategy: decide what is non negotiable, delegate or drop the rest and build in recovery. That might mean batch cooking on a Sunday, saying yes to one social event and no to two, or asking family to share the load. Your future self will be grateful.

Look for small calm moments through the day. A cup of tea without the phone, a walk at lunchtime, three slow breaths before a meeting. They sound trivial. They add up, because your nervous system responds to regular signals of safety.
