The science

Why midlife and parties do not mix like they used to

Many women find alcohol disrupts sleep more than it used to, and some notice it can trigger hot flushes. Late nights, rich food and less movement compound the effect. Eating before you go out, alternating drinks with water, and protecting your sleep on the nights in between make a real difference.

Before the event, eat something with protein. Choose your drinks deliberately, alternate with water and set a time to go home. Afterwards, take a short walk the next morning and have a proper breakfast. These small habits keep blood sugar steadier and sleep less disturbed.

If you are hosting, make the food easy and the evening short. If you are going out, give yourself permission to leave early. Being present matters more than lasting the distance.
