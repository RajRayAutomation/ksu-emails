Picture a teacher we will call David. By November he is forgetting words, losing his keys and rereading the same email three times.

It is not a lack of intelligence. It is often a lack of light, sleep and sometimes nutrients.

When the brain is tired, the basics matter most.

The science

The basics of a clearer head

Sleep, daylight, movement, hydration and regular meals do most of the work. Choline, found in eggs, fish and liver, is the building block your body uses to make acetylcholine, a messenger involved in memory and attention. Many people eat fewer choline rich foods than they realise. A supplement can help fill the gap alongside a good diet.

A brain friendly breakfast might be eggs with vegetables, or full fat yoghurt with nuts and berries. These give you protein, healthy fat and fibre for steady energy. Add a glass of water and your coffee, and you have given your brain a decent start.

On days when the fog is heavy, resist the urge to add more caffeine. Take a short walk, drink water, eat something and try again in twenty minutes. Often what you need is fuel and movement, not another cup.
