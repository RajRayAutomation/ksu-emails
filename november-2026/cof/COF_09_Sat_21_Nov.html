L Theanine and caffeine

L Theanine is an amino acid found naturally in tea and associated with relaxed alertness. Studies of L Theanine taken together with caffeine suggest a smoother effect on attention than caffeine alone for some people, with fewer jitters. Individual response varies, and it works best alongside good sleep and regular meals.
