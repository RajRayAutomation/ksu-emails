The science

Caffeine and sleep

Caffeine blocks adenosine, the chemical that builds sleep pressure through the day. Because it lingers for hours, an afternoon coffee can reduce how deep and refreshing your sleep is, even if you fall asleep without trouble. People vary a lot in sensitivity. If you wake tired, moving your last caffeinated cup earlier is one of the simplest things to test.

If you want to keep the afternoon ritual without the caffeine, plan it. A warm mug, a short walk, ten minutes without screens. The ritual is half of what you are enjoying, so keep it.

Notice how you sleep after a week of this change. Some people feel the difference in days, others in two or three weeks. If you notice no change, you have lost nothing and you have a lovely cup of decaf.
