The science

Why altitude matters

Coffee grown at altitude ripens slowly in cooler air. Slower ripening tends to give denser beans with more developed sweetness and acidity, which is why high grown coffees are prized. Shade, soil and careful processing add to the picture. It is also why the same variety can taste completely different from one hillside to the next.

When you brew Aponte Inga, take a moment to smell the grounds first. Notice the sweetness and the depth. Then taste the finished cup slowly. Coffee is one of the few daily pleasures that rewards paying attention.

Knowing where your coffee comes from changes how it tastes. It becomes a small connection to people and a place far away. That is part of what we love about it, and we hope you do too.
