The science

Caffeine, cortisol and timing

Cortisol naturally rises in the first hour after waking, which already helps alertness. Some people find that waiting sixty to ninety minutes for their first coffee leads to a smoother day, while others notice little difference. The research is mixed, so treat it as an experiment, not a rule. What is better supported is keeping caffeine away from the afternoon if your sleep is suffering.

If you want to test the delay, start gently. Have a glass of water, get your light, do a few minutes of movement, then make your coffee. Notice whether you feel steadier mid morning. If you feel worse, or you simply like your coffee early, go back. There is no prize for suffering.

What matters more than timing is the total. Most people do well with one to three cups, finished by early afternoon. If you feel jittery or anxious, or you are sleeping badly, cutting back is a good place to start.
