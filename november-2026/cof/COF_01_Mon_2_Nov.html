The science

Why light before coffee helps

Morning light, even on a grey day, is a stronger signal to your body clock than anything in a cup. Getting outside, or at least beside a bright window, for a few minutes after waking helps set alertness for the day and supports sleep that night. Coffee then does a better job of the rest.

A simple morning looks like this. Wake, drink a glass of water, put on a coat and go outside for five minutes. Come back, make your coffee and add your scoop. Sit down without your phone for the first few sips. It takes ten minutes and sets up the rest of the day.

On very dark or wet mornings, a bright lamp by the kitchen window helps. It is not the same as outdoor light, but it is better than a dim room. The aim is to give your body clock a clear signal as early as you can.
